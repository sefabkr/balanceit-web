A moment of balance

Balance It

A calm physics stacking game on a porcelain sea. Stack wood, stone and glazed pieces, keep the tower steady, and watch it shine on the water at dawn, at sunset or by moonlight. For iPhone and iPad, and made for iPhone Duo. Requires iOS 15 or later.

Coming to the App Store

How to play

Move and placePress the piece and drag it over the dais. Lift your finger to set it down.
TurnHold the piece still for a moment, then drag to turn it.
Look aroundSwipe sideways on empty water to circle the tower. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
DropA short tap drops the piece where it is. The balance mark under the score shows how steady the tower is.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. You earn coins by playing and spend them on boosts (wide base, slow motion, undo) and on new piece collections.

What can I play?

Levels give you a goal height and a budget of pieces; from level 6, some levels add a twist such as wind, a narrow dais or fragile glass. Endless lets you build as high as you can. The Daily Tower gives everyone the same ten pieces each day, with one scored attempt. On an open iPhone Duo, Duel puts two towers side by side for two players.

The first-run lesson

A short lesson shows each gesture on your first run. You can see it again from Settings › How to play › Show the lesson again.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your tallest tower and your Daily Tower go to the leaderboards, and achievements unlock as you play. Game Center is optional; the game plays the same without it.

Accessibility

Every button has a VoiceOver label, and the board accepts direct touch while VoiceOver is on. Menu text follows your Larger Text setting. Reduce Motion stops camera shake and swooping moves. Haptics can be switched off in Settings.

Is my progress saved?

Your levels, stars, coins, collections and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Which languages are there?

17: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português, Nederlands, Polski, Русский, Українська, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. The game follows your device’s language; you can pick another one in the game’s Settings › Language.
