Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Applies to Balance It for iPhone and iPad

Short version: Balance It collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no servers of its own to talk to. If you use Game Center, Apple handles it.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, share or sell any personal information or usage data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). The settings screen lists optional analytics and personalised ads; both are off and not part of this version.

2. What stays on your device

To keep your game, the app stores on your device:

  • your progress: the level you reached, stars, coins, boosts, unlocked collections, your best heights and your Daily Tower streak;
  • your settings: music, sound, haptics, language, the hour of the porcelain sea you chose, and whether you have seen the lesson.

This never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

3. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Apple’s Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. Apple processes this under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your name, Apple Account or any other personal information. Game Center is optional.

4. Sharing a tower

When you tap Share, the app draws a picture of your tower on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

5. iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo the game reads whether the phone is open, half open or closed, to lay out the screen and offer the two-player Duel. This happens on the device while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

6. Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

7. Changes

If a future version ever changes what the app does with information, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
